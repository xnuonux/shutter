## Reusable workflow supported by this test

1. Keep original identity and current story condition separate.
2. Compose an opening still with the exact camera, characters, condition, location and prop arrangement needed for that shot.
3. Inspect the still before animation. A flawed still should not become a paid video request just because the model can animate it.
4. Use image-to-video for a shot whose composition is settled. Use reference-to-video when the desired operation needs separate identities, motion, voice or scene references.
5. Compare separate takes, retain their actual inputs and charges, then assemble selected footage with sound. Selection for a review cut does not certify every continuity criterion.

This is the preferred next workflow for similarly constrained shots, supported by these two examples. It does not establish universal superiority, exact 3D geometry, all-frame anatomy, voice consistency or episode-scale reliability. The new viewpoints were made with the image tool, not Blender; no geometric camera path was measured. High precision camera choreography remains a separate test.
